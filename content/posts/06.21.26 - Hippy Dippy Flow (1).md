Weekly yoga flow #1 complete. As usual, the flow was completed to Tritonal’s Reverence album. There are 14 tracks, and I try to complete one asana or flow per track. Ideally they flow together, but not always. This one ended up with a good number of hip openers and a bound warrior II which I haven’t tried in a long time. It came together nicely though I didn’t focus much on transitions and wish I had. In watching the video back. I’m struck but not surprised by how much I round at mid back. 

1. Low lunges (anjaneyasana)
2. Child’s pose (balasana) 
3. Frog pose, then with thread the needle pose (mandukasana + parsva balasana) 
4. Deer pose (mrigasana)
5. Goddess pose to low squat (utkata konasana + malasana)
6. Very slow sun salutation A (10 breaths per pose) (surya namaskar) 
7. Slow (10 breaths per pose) Surya namaskar + low and high crescent lunge (ashta chandrasana) + warrior II (virabhadrasana II) + reverse triangle (viparita trikonasana) + bound warrior II (virabhadrasana II)  
8. Repeat #7 but move with the breath
9. Tree pose variations (vrksasana) 
10. Kundalini-inspired free movement 
11. Half-bow and bow pose (dhanurasana) sequence + locust pose (salabhasana) 
12. Back and leg stretches
13. Shoulder stand (salamba sarvangasana) + plow (halasana) + salamba sarvangasana with hands raised to knees + boat (navasana) 
14. Savasana
